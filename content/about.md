## What this site is

An independent, unofficial guide to Grand Theft Auto VI. It is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

## How we use sources {#how-we-label-facts}

We link Rockstar and platform documentation for release details and controls. For mission objectives we link the walkthrough, timestamp or player discussion behind the answer. When a tip comes from one player, we say where they encountered it. A rumor links back to the person or outlet making the claim.

Mission guides come in two kinds, and each page says which it is.

- **Tested.** We played the mission ourselves. The page shows the console and the test date.
- **Community-sourced.** We wrote the steps in our own words from public player reports and videos. Every source is linked. Each source must show or state play of the released game, and sources that only repeat each other count as one. The page says it is not tested by us and whether a person has reviewed the source check. We replace it after we play the mission.

Creator clips link to the original upload. Wasted Loop videos made for this site include a transcript. Preview coverage stays separate from tested walkthroughs. We leave leaks out of guides.

## Corrections

To report a mistake, use the "Report a problem with this guide" link at the bottom of any guide. It opens a public form on GitHub and needs a free GitHub account. Anyone can read what you post there, so leave out game codes, account names and personal details.

Guide corrections appear on the affected page. We update the test date after replaying an affected step and link the source for changes to platform or support details.

## Advertising and affiliate disclosure

If a store link may earn us a commission, we disclose it next to that link. We choose purchase options by compatibility and whether they solve the problem.

## Sponsor a guide {#sponsorships}

A launch sponsor can request a labeled placement beside an original mission walkthrough or a Wasted Loop short. For example, the [Lab Fees guide](/missions/lab-fees/) reaches readers looking up a keypad code, while the [taxi fare video](/taxi-video/) helps players decide whether to skip a ride.

To check availability, [send a sponsorship inquiry on GitHub](https://github.com/shiestyslime/gtasixmissions/issues/new?title=Sponsorship%20inquiry&body=Brand%20or%20project%3A%20%0ACompany%20website%3A%20%0AGuide%20of%20interest%3A%20%0ACampaign%20dates%3A%20). Include your brand’s website, the guide you have in mind and your campaign dates. The inquiry is public and requires a GitHub account. Keep private contact and payment details out of it.

Sponsorship buys an identified advertising placement. Editors choose the recommendations and write the verdict.
