## How do you progress in GTA 6?

{reported} You progress by finishing story missions. Some chapters also need a set amount of cash before they open. Rockstar has said mission pay alone is not enough.

In plain terms: play missions, earn extra money in the open world, then return to the story.

## The progression loop

1. Play the available story missions.
2. Reach a chapter that needs cash.
3. Earn money through robberies, heists and sales. See [money-making](/money-making/).
4. Bank the money and unlock the next chapter.

## Wanted level

- {reported} The wanted system has **six stars**. GTA 5 had five.
- {unknown} What each star level sends after you.

A high wanted level puts carried cash and loot at risk. Lose the police before you travel to a fence or a bank.

## Character growth and customization

- {reported} Character customization is deeper than in earlier games. Previews show a gym, clothing and appearance changes.

## Side activities

{reported} The Extended Look shows basketball, scuba diving, gym workouts, jet skis, kayaks, dirt bikes, races and arcade machines.

{reported} There are 62 bird species to spot with binoculars.

{reported} The game has optional ways to be kind. Rockstar's Rob Nelson says they are there for players who want them. One example: Jason can ask a dog's owner if he can pet the dog. Nelson also says your actions have some effect on how the game plays out.

## Where do I start the story? {#start-the-story}

Start with Lucia in the Prologue. Gone Fishing follows and puts you in control of Jason. Choose your current objective in the [mission guides](/missions/) to get help without reading ahead. {src:5,6}

## FAQ

### Do you need money to unlock story missions in GTA 6?

Yes, for some chapters, according to previews. You need a set amount of cash before they unlock.

### How many wanted stars are in GTA 6?

Six. The Extended Look video shows a six-star wanted level.

### Is there a level or XP system in GTA 6 story mode?

Not yet known. Rockstar has not described one.

### Does GTA 6 punish you for causing chaos?

Rockstar has not said so. It told The Guardian that the kinder options are optional, and that your actions have some effect on how the game plays out. The details are not yet known.

### Can you keep playing after the GTA 6 story ends?

Not yet confirmed. Earlier GTA games let you free roam after the story.

### Is there GTA 6 Online at launch?

Rockstar has not announced an online mode or its date.
