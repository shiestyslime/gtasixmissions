## How do you progress in GTA 6?

{reported} You progress by finishing story missions. Some chapters also need a set amount of cash before they open. Rockstar has said mission pay alone is not enough.

In plain terms: play missions, earn extra money in the open world, then return to the story.

## No new mission showing on the map? {#no-mission-showing}

A player in the launch-day [Reddit mission thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/GTA6/comments/1xzltf4/comment/19su0m5heexhg/) reached an “explore” prompt without an obvious next job. Here’s where to look first.

**Check the map legend.** PowerPyx identifies main-story starts as pink markers with black letters. A map label can differ from the title that appears when the mission actually starts. Early on, for example, **Go See Cal** starts **Unwanted Attention**. [Look up the objective in the mission index](/missions/). {src:7,8}

**Check the in-game time.** If a contact or marker tells you to return later, look at the clock on your phone. Some missions are available only at a particular time. VGC describes using sleep at Jason’s house to advance time. {src:9}

**If the marker needs both leads**, open the map and look for the prompt to text the other character. GameSpot’s reviewer used this to arrange a meet-up at the mission. {src:14}

**If you’re waiting on a call**, continue with available activities. PowerPyx found that some contacts in optional quest lines call back after other tasks or more story progress. The timing depends on the contact. {src:7}

## When can I switch characters? {#switch-characters}

In Chapter 2, **Breakfast Run** leads directly into **Coffee Break**. Finish the partner tutorial there. Hold right on the D-pad for the partner menu, then press **R1 on PS5** or **RB on Xbox** when the switch prompt appears. Some story objectives choose the character for you. {src:10}

## Where can I see character strength? {#check-strength}

On PS5, open the touchpad menu, select **Jason & Lucia**, then select a character. Scroll to the heart icon to see that character’s Strength. To find a gym, use the map’s Activities filter and look for a dumbbell. {src:11}

## Wanted level

Rockstar’s Extended Look shows a six-star wanted meter. Early in the story, the [Phoenix impound objective](/missions/unwanted-attention/#pay-n-spray) gives you a practical example. The next destination appears after the chase ends. {src:3,8}

## Side activities

{reported} The Extended Look shows basketball, scuba diving, gym workouts, jet skis, kayaks, dirt bikes, races and arcade machines.

{reported} There are 62 bird species to spot with binoculars.

{reported} The game has optional ways to be kind. Rockstar's Rob Nelson says they are there for players who want them. One example: Jason can ask a dog's owner if he can pet the dog. Nelson also says your actions have some effect on how the game plays out.

## After the credits: where is the 100% checklist? {#after-the-story}

You can return to free roam after the finale. Open **Progress → Stats → 100% Completion** to see the game’s checklist. If you want to finish side activities, you can continue on this save. If you want to play an earlier story mission again, load a separate [manual save made before it](/save-game/#mission-replay). {src:12,13}

## Where do I start the story? {#start-the-story}

Start with Lucia in the Prologue. Gone Fishing follows and puts you in control of Jason. Choose your current objective in the [mission guides](/missions/) to get help without reading ahead. {src:5,6}

## FAQ

### Do you need money to unlock story missions in GTA 6?

Yes, for some chapters, according to previews. You need a set amount of cash before they unlock.

### How many wanted stars are in GTA 6?

Six. The Extended Look video shows a six-star wanted level.

### Can you keep playing after the GTA 6 story ends?

Yes. Kotaku’s reviewer finished the game and returned to free roam. The [postgame checklist](#after-the-story) tracks the activities you can finish afterward. {src:12,13}
