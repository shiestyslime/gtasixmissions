## Where is GTA 6 set?

{confirmed} GTA 6 is set in present-day Leonida, a fictional US state based on Florida. Its biggest city is Vice City, which is based on Miami.

## The six confirmed regions

| Region | Type of place | Real-world inspiration |
|---|---|---|
| Vice City | Big coastal city | Miami |
| Leonida Keys | Island chain | Florida Keys |
| Grassrivers | Wetlands and swamp | Everglades |
| Port Gellhorn | Faded coastal town | Florida Panhandle |
| Ambrosia | Industrial town | Sugar country near Lake Okeechobee |
| Mount Kalaga National Park | Forest and wilderness | Northern Florida and the southern Appalachians |

The inspiration column is our reading of Rockstar's descriptions. Rockstar does not name real places.

### Vice City

{confirmed} The main city. Rockstar shows beaches, art deco hotels, a busy port, an airport and nightlife areas. Expect most shops, clubs and city missions here.

### Leonida Keys

{confirmed} A chain of small islands with bars, boats and open water. Jason starts the story living here.

### Grassrivers

{confirmed} A large wetland with swamps, airboats and dangerous wildlife such as alligators.

### Port Gellhorn

{confirmed} A worn-down coastal town with cheap motels, closed attractions and off-road racing.

### Ambrosia

{confirmed} An industrial town built around a sugar refinery. A biker gang is active here.

### Mount Kalaga National Park

{confirmed} A wild area near the state's northern border. Rockstar shows hunting, fishing and off-road trails.

## FAQ

### Where does GTA 6 take place?

GTA 6 takes place in the fictional state of Leonida, based on Florida. The main city is Vice City, based on Miami.

### Is Vice City in GTA 6?

Yes. Vice City is the main city in GTA 6. It is a modern version, not the 1980s city from GTA: Vice City.

### How many regions are in GTA 6?

Rockstar has named six regions: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. More areas may exist in the full game.

Rockstar has not stated a size. Comparisons before launch are estimates.
