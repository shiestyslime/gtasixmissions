## What we collect

We do not ask you to create an account or give personal details. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security.

Search downloads the public guide index and matches your query in the browser. The storage checker calculates the result in your browser. Neither tool sends the text or numbers you enter to the site.

## Advertising

If advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to show ads based on your earlier visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads at [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

## Fonts

This site loads one font from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google's servers to download it.

## Affiliate links

We label links that may earn a commission. External stores have their own privacy and cookie policies.

## Changes

We update this page when our practices change. The date at the top shows the latest change.
