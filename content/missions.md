## Is there a GTA 6 mission list?

Press Start’s Brodie Gibbons lists all the titles he saw in Story Recap. PC Gamer published a 91-mission list and hid three names for spoilers. PowerPyx also tracks smaller objectives and optional jobs. Two checklists can put a different number beside the same mission. Search by its **name or map label**. {src:6,7,8}

For side jobs in the first two chapters, see [Chapter 1 errands (spoilers)](/chapter-1-missions/) and [Chapter 2 errands (spoilers)](/chapter-2-missions/). For later objectives, open the [Chapter 3 guide (spoilers)](/chapter-3-missions/), [Chapter 4 marker guide (spoilers)](/chapter-4-missions/) or [Chapter 5 finale guide (spoilers)](/chapter-5-missions/).

## Find an objective

Search for the mission title or a short phrase from the objective on your screen. Choose the matching step for the immediate action. Check the recovery note if the objective fails to advance. Tested guides list the platform and date. Community-sourced guides link every source next to the step it supports. Both keep plot details apart from the steps you need to play.

## Retry a checkpoint or revisit a finished mission {#checkpoint-or-replay}

**Still in the mission?** GamesRadar’s reviewer describes manually retrying the most recent checkpoint during a mission. Use this when you want another attempt at the current section. {src:5}

**Already finished the mission?** PowerPyx’s launch guide says it found no option to replay a completed mission from a list. If you want to revisit a chapter later, its advice is to keep a separate manual save before moving on. Keep your current progress in its own slot. [Here’s where to find Save Game on PS5 and Xbox](/save-game/). {src:6}

If you can see the objective but have lost the route line, [check your minimap and GPS settings](/navigation-settings/#change-the-minimap).

## How missions work

{confirmed} GTA VI is a single-player game following Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Rockstar's official gameplay preview shows both characters in action.

{reported} In a preview for The Guardian, Rockstar showed a robbery with advance scouting and a choice of ways to deal with security. Use the guide for your current objective to see which prompts actually advance it.

## How long is the story?

{reported} Rockstar North's Rob Nelson said his own run took about 80 hours and included optional goals. That time includes side activities.

## FAQ

### How many missions are in GTA 6?

Press Start counted **93** in the game’s Story Recap. PC Gamer lists **91**. Optional jobs and smaller map tasks make the playable checklist longer than either story count. {src:6,7,8}

### What should I search if I'm stuck on a mission?

Type the mission name or a short phrase from the objective shown on your screen. Open the matching step and follow its recovery note if the objective still won't advance.

### Can I replay a finished mission in GTA 6?

PowerPyx found no completed-mission replay option in its launch playthrough. GamesRadar describes retrying the last checkpoint while the mission is still active. [See the save advice](/missions/#checkpoint-or-replay). {src:5,6}
