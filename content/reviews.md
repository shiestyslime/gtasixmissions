## Are GTA 6 reviews out?

{reported} Yes. Reviews went live on November 18, 2026, one day before launch. As of that day, Metacritic and OpenCritic both show a score of 99. Scores can still move as more reviews arrive.

> These scores come from the aggregator sites and the outlets' own pages. They are not our own review. We have not played the game yet.

## When do GTA 6 reviews come out?

- {reported} The embargo lifted on **November 18, 2026, at 6 a.m. Eastern**, according to Kotaku. Reviews from Game Informer and Vice are timestamped at that hour.
- {confirmed} The game releases on November 19, 2026.

## FAQ

### What is GTA 6's Metacritic score?

99 for the PS5 version, from 147 reviews on November 18, 2026. The score can still change as more reviews are added.

### What is GTA 6's OpenCritic score?

A Top Critic Average of 99, with 99% of critics recommending it, from 81 reviews on November 18, 2026.

### When is the GTA 6 review embargo?

It lifted on November 18, 2026, at 6 a.m. Eastern, one day before launch, according to Kotaku.

### Do reviews mention performance problems?

Some do. Cultured Vultures found the frame rate rough in places on a base PS5. The game targets 30 frames per second on every console.

### What did GTA 5 score on Metacritic?

GTA 5 scored 97. Red Dead Redemption 2 also scored 97, and GTA 4 scored 98.
