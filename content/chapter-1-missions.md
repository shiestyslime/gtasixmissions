These are the errands and smaller objectives included alongside named missions on the [Chapter 1 checklist](/missions/#catalogue-chapter-1). The instructions come from PowerPyx’s individual playthroughs. The source number beside each entry opens the relevant walkthrough.

## Get Shirley’s Rum {#get-shirleys-rum}

When Pipework becomes available, listen for Jason’s neighbor Shirley in Key Lento. Accept her request, visit the clerk at Three Sheets Liquor and bring the rum back. Starting [Tourist Trap](/missions/tourist-trap/) closes this favor. Missing it leaves trophy and overall completion eligibility intact. {src:1}

## Steal the Scooter {#steal-the-scooter}

Revisit Shirley about an in-game day after delivering the rum. When she asks a second favor, collect Peggy’s scooter and drive it **inside Shirley’s porch** to finish. Complete it before Tourist Trap if you want to see the scene. {src:2}

## Pick up Groceries & Drop Off Groceries {#pick-up-groceries-drop-off-groceries}

In the marked Key Lento shop, choose any three products with pink objective markers. Put the bag in your trunk and close it. At Jason’s house, take the bag back out and carry it inside to trigger the handoff. {src:3}

## Buy Flowers {#buy-flowers}

Visit the flower stand in southern Key Lento. The $10 bouquet and $25 roses both count. The seller arranges delivery, so Jason can leave once he has paid. {src:4}

## Buy Lucia a Necklace {#buy-lucia-a-necklace}

The Siren’s Chest is beside the barber in southern Key Lento. Speak to the clerk and buy the $475 necklace if you want the gift. Chapter 2 consequence: Lucia can later use it as collateral at Starlet Motel. {src:5}

## Get The Chop {#get-the-chop}

Visit Barnacle Beauties next to the jewelry shop. Any new haircut you buy completes the task. Pick one you want to keep for a while, since changing back later costs money again. {src:6}

## Get Lucia’s Clothes {#get-lucias-clothes}

After Feng Shui, answer Andy’s call from an unknown number. At the Manatee’s Rest, ask the bartender for Andy and follow her to her car. Stow the clothing bag in your trunk and take that car to collect Lucia. {src:7}

## Deposit Cash Into BuckMe {#deposit-cash-into-buckme}

After [Buck Me](/missions/buck-me/), open the BuckMe app and select **ATM Finder – Set Destination**. Use the machine it marks to deposit carried cash. The task itself has no destination marker until you set one. {src:8}

## Save for the Future {#save-for-the-future}

Use the BuckMe app to transfer money from checking into Family Savings. This is an ongoing goal across the story. The first savings target is $5,000. Transfers to Family Savings are permanent, so leave spending money in checking. {src:9}

## Find the Lost Drugs {#find-the-lost-drugs}

After Down to Earth, the call adds four search zones. Listen for beeps and watch for a yellow flash when searching. At Thorpe Adventures, the empty package outside is a clue. The pickup that completes that area is in the shed. {src:10}

## Clock Out {#clock-out}

If Lucia calls after Day Release, accept the request and reach Key Lento Community Center in a vehicle **before 4 p.m. in-game**. You can check the time on Jason’s phone. Drive her home to finish the favor. {src:11}

## Building a Life {#building-a-life}

This starts at Jason’s house immediately after Clear Skies. Interact with the left side of the unfinished coffee table. If the game asks you to undo the screws, follow the prompt. The glass top is in the cardboard box behind Lucia. {src:12}

## Donnie’s Lost Drugs {#donnies-lost-drugs}

After Swamped, go to the offshore marker north of Key Lento. In the southeast part of the pink search area, look for the sunken boat on the seabed. The quest bag is in its rear compartment. {src:13}
