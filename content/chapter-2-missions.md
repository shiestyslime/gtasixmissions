Match the wording on your phone or map to an entry below. These are the smaller objectives listed alongside the main guides on the [Chapter 2 checklist](/missions/#catalogue-chapter-2). Each numbered source links to the individual PowerPyx playthrough.

## Take Care of Jason {#take-care-of-jason}

After What’s in the Bag?, check on Jason at Starlet Motel between other jobs. The task lives under **Map → Story** and can stay open across several visits. “Play Nurse” is the name shown during a care scene. The ongoing objective clears after [Breakfast Run](/missions/breakfast-run/). {src:1}

## Overnight Charges {#overnight-charges}

Leave your room and enter reception on the left. Talk to Linda. If Jason bought the necklace in Chapter 1, Lucia uses it as collateral. While you’re at the desk, inspect the wall map to reveal Port Gellhorn on your own map. {src:2}

## Pick Up A Gun {#pick-up-a-gun}

After Girls’ Trip, the optional marker leads to Gellhorn Outfitters in Port Gellhorn. If you already own an item in the weapons locker, using the green locker to retrieve it counts for the objective. {src:3}

## New Look {#new-look}

After Girls’ Trip, go to Pearl House Hair Salon in Port Gellhorn. Speak to the clerk and buy a new hairstyle for Lucia. Any of the available hairstyles completes the task. {src:4}

## How Do I Look? {#how-do-i-look}

At Bargain World in southern Port Gellhorn, buy both parts of an outfit for Lucia. Pick a top and a pair of pants or shorts. Buying a top alone leaves the task open. {src:5}

## Painkiller Supplier {#painkiller-supplier}

There’s no marker to drive to. Keep working on the other Port Gellhorn jobs after [Girls’ Trip](/missions/girls-trip/). Dunk’s first call changes the entry to **Dunk Will Be in Touch**. His later call unlocks [Slam Dunk](/missions/slam-dunk/). {src:6}

## Check Out McKittrick Farm {#check-out-mckittrick-farm}

Read the flyer on the outside wall of Starlet Motel reception. It marks the airstrip east of the motel. Wait for Kyle to land and get out of his plane, then speak to him and accept. Talk to him again after the flight for the $400 reward. {src:7}

## Behind on Payments {#behind-on-payments}

Return to Linda at the Starlet Motel front desk with **$900** to settle the room bill. You can leave the balance outstanding while you finish other jobs and earn the money. {src:8}

## Collect Chemicals for Kyle {#collect-chemicals-for-kyle}

After the first farm job, watch for Kyle’s text about a warehouse in Leonard County. The delivery finishes inside the barn at his farm. Park the mission van there to trigger his inspection. {src:9}

## Hit Avery Pawn {#hit-avery-pawn}

Finishing Coffee Break adds the optional Avery Pawn marker in Port Gellhorn. If you finish with a bag of quest valuables, Dunk’s trailer is the location where you can exchange the bag for in-game money. {src:10}

## Hit Kingpin Bowling {#hit-kingpin-bowling}

Kingpin Bowling appears after Coffee Break. For the **Let’s Go Bowling** trophy, the game counts three separate valuables. PowerPyx found the luxury watch in the southeast room. If you miss it, the location can reappear later in the story. {src:11}

## Impounded Vehicle {#impounded-vehicle}

After Breakfast Run, look for Lucia’s green car icon near the Port Gellhorn police station. Coffee Break adds the recovery task to the map. Later, the Scooter Bros. phone app can retrieve an impounded personal car for a fee. {src:12}

## Prepare for Vice City {#prepare-for-vice-city}

This is an earnings target across jobs. Reach **$15,000** and complete the remaining main objectives under **Map → Story**. When the motel room gets its marker, return there to start [Checking Out](/missions/checking-out/). {src:13}

## Veteran Hunted {#veteran-hunted}

Play as Jason after Coffee Break and watch for a text from Bret Vogel. His marker is in Lummox County, far north of the motel. Arrive as Jason **without Lucia** to start the hunting tutorial. {src:14}
