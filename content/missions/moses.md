## When can I register a car? {#registration-unlock}

Jason can visit an auto shop in Chapter 1, but registering a different personal car comes later. Play through Lucia’s introduction to Moses in Chapter 2. His garage can register a suitable vehicle so it stays yours across journeys. {src:1,3}

## Why won’t Moses accept my vehicle? {#eligible-vehicle}

Check the tracker status the game displays during Moses’s tutorial. A car shown as untracked is eligible at this point in the story. Even after registration opens, cars with advanced trackers are still outside his capabilities. For a later registration, check the amount the garage asks before confirming. {src:1,2,3}
