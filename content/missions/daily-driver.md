## Which car matches Moses’s picture? {#white-crawler}

Check the picture in Moses’s message before entering the search area. The vehicle is a white off-roader parked in the open lot by the old Trader’s Union building. It’s the Crawler that matches the photo. {src:1,3}

## Where do I deliver the Crawler? {#delivery-garage}

Return to Moses’s garage in Port Gellhorn. This is a delivery for the mechanic who just taught Lucia about personal vehicles, so take this specific car to him. {src:1,2,3}
