## Where is the ambulance marker taking me? {#find-the-barn}

Start outside the motel after the opening bag scene. Look for the barn on the east side of the property. The ambulance is inside, out of view from the road. {src:1,3}

## Why is the ambulance leaving? {#moving-ambulance}

Reaching the vehicle triggers an encounter with people who found it first. Once they take off, the mission follows that same ambulance. There’s no second ambulance to find back at the hospital. {src:1,2,3}
