## Where do we go after Breakfast Run? {#motel-kitchen}

Go to the kitchen at Starlet Motel with Lucia. The two characters meet there to discuss their next jobs in Kelly County. This conversation leads into their joint free-roam objectives. {src:1,2}

## How do I switch to Jason or Lucia? {#character-switch}

Continue through Coffee Break until the game introduces partner controls. Use the right direction on the D-pad to bring up the option to change characters. Reaching the motel at the start of Chapter 2 gives you access to Lucia earlier than it gives you this free-switch option. {src:1,3}
