## What unlocks Billy’s invitation? {#race-first}

Follow Wyman’s lead to Thrillbilly Mud Club and complete the race with Billy’s crew. The invitation for this job comes after that introduction. {src:1,2}

## Is this another race at the mud club? {#follow-billy}

This time the objective is to join Billy and ride with his crew in Kelly County. Follow Billy as the mission begins. You’re on a group ride toward a story encounter, so there’s no finish-line position to chase. {src:1,2}
