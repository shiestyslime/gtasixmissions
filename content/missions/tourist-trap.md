## Can I go back to free-roam between these missions? {#chapter-one-chain}

Finish any free-roam errands you want to do first. Tourist Trap leads directly into [Home Sweet Home](/missions/home-sweet-home/) and then [Discharge Time](/missions/discharge-time/). Free-roam pauses during that sequence. {src:1,2}

## Where do I go after collecting the bag? {#visitor-center-lockers}

Find the visitor center’s locker area on the upper walkway and interact with the locker to take the bag. Follow the return objective downstairs. The next scene begins on the way back. {src:1,2}

## What counts for the dock objective? {#dock-objective}

Stay with your companion as you move toward the docks. Bring him to the waiting boat when the assistance prompt appears. Once on board, follow the open-water objective to continue. {src:1,2}
