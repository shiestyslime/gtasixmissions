## Where does Discharge Time start? {#hospital-start}

Finish [Home Sweet Home](/missions/home-sweet-home/) to reach the hospital automatically. Lucia starts the search here. Head upstairs to the second floor and enter Jason’s room to continue. {src:1,2}

## Who controls the wheelchair sequence? {#character-controls}

When Lucia begins moving the wheelchair, player control switches to Jason. Lucia handles the route through the hospital in this section. Later, when Jason is on the floor, use the movement control to crawl toward the item named in the objective. {src:1,2}

## Do I need to drive the ambulance? {#ambulance-objective}

In the final sequence you control Jason in the back of the ambulance. The vehicle keeps moving while you handle the on-screen defense objective. Completing that sequence closes Chapter 1. {src:1,2}
