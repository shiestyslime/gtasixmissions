## Where is Cal's marker? {#go-see-cal}

Finish [Gone Fishing](/missions/gone-fishing/) as Jason. Look north of Jason's house for the **C** on the map. Set a waypoint there. Once you've spoken to Cal, take his truck and follow the route to Hamlet. {src:1,3}

## Which car is the Phoenix? {#find-the-phoenix}

1. Look for the red sports car attached to the tow truck. GTA Series Videos shows [the rear of the Phoenix at 5:30](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m2APlrqUlqY&t=330s). You can also scan the car with Jason’s phone to check its name. {src:1,2,3,5}
2. Get into the tow truck first and use its release control. Once the Phoenix is detached, change vehicles and follow the prompt inside the red car. {src:1,2,3}

### Can I leave in the tow truck?

Switch into the Phoenix once you release it. Players in the launch-day mission thread describe taking the wrong vehicle here. Check the red car’s name on Jason’s phone before taking it to the next marker. {src:1,2,4}

## When does the Pay 'n' Spray appear? {#pay-n-spray}

The respray objective comes after the wanted-level objective. Keep the Phoenix drivable. Once the chase ends, follow the new marker to the Pay 'n' Spray in Hamlet and select a body color. The next destination is Brian's garage. {src:1,2,3}

## Why is the mission still active after the drop-off? {#after-delivery}

Parking the Phoenix completes the delivery. Jason still has a cash pickup and a clothing purchase to make. Collect the money at the garage, then follow the clothing objective to the nearby gun shop and equip an outfit. {src:1,2,3}

## FAQ

### Which mission adds the police scanner to Jason's phone?

Unwanted Attention. Cal installs the scanner when you reach the Hamlet impound. It lets you hear the game's police radio. {src:1,2,3}

### How much cash do you collect at the garage?

The pickup is $150 in game currency. You can use it to cover the outfit objective that follows. {src:1,3}
