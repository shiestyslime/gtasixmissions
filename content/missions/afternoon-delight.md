## Which of Vicki’s locations starts Afternoon Delight? {#meet-at-delights}

Return to her workplace, Delights Cabaret in Port Gellhorn. This is Vicki’s later job for Jason and Lucia, separate from meeting her at her motel room in Girls’ Trip. {src:1,2}

## Who is Mario? {#meet-mario}

Mario is the performer at the club on ladies’ night. His group is part of the mission encounter once Jason and Lucia are at Delights. {src:1,2}
