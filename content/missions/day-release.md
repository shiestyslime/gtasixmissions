## Which jet ski should I take? {#jet-ski-choice}

Travel to the yacht by speedboat. When you have the choice of riding a jet ski alone or with Jason, choosing to ride together gives the relationship a boost. Follow the race course back to the yacht. {src:1,2}

## Where is Mattia after Lucia changes clothes? {#find-mattia}

Pick a dress, then leave the cabin and work down through the yacht. Mattia is below the deck where you started. Reach him to change scenes and start following his car. {src:1,2}

## Where do I go for Find Jason? {#find-jason}

After the driving and club sections, Lucia starts the search inside an apartment building. Follow the long hallway to its far end. That is where the next interaction begins. {src:1,2}
