## I’m at the liquor store. Where is Arnold? {#radar-station}

The liquor store is Cal’s pickup. Wait for Cal to get in with Brian, then drive to the next mission area. Head for the radar station to trigger the meeting with Arnold. {src:1,2}

## The chase route changes at the bridge {#chase-route}

Following Arnold in the car takes you over a stunt jump at the broken bridge. If Arnold gets out later, continue on foot. On this route the game gives Jason a decision when he catches up. You can make that choice without looking up the ending. {src:1,2}
