## Which furniture do I move next? {#furniture-order}

Start inside Jason’s house. Get the beer and sit with Cal to begin the room rearrangement. Start with the TV. The coffee table comes next, followed by the couch and then the chair. Stay with Cal afterward for the console games. {src:1,2}

## Do I need to beat Street Trash III? {#console-games}

The console starts with **Tenebrae Lux**. Play until Jason asks to change games. In **Street Trash III**, a loss still advances the mission. If the game ends, let the following scene play. {src:1,2}
