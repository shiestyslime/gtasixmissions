## Which object should I look at first? {#first-object}

Object spoilers follow. At the beginning of Chapter 2 you control Lucia at the motel. The small statue is the object to examine when the game asks you to read a note. The note is attached to the underside. {src:1,3}

## Where do I take the hard drive? {#hard-drive}

Stay in the room for this objective. The drive is the second item the pair look at together. Examining it completes the inspection sequence. {src:1,2,3}
