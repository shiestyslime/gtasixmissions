## Where is Wyman sending us? {#meet-billy}

Wyman is the contact who mentions prize money. Billy is the racer you meet at Thrillbilly Mud Club. Follow the club lead to get into his race. {src:1,2}

## Which job follows the race? {#billys-next-job}

The club race introduces Billy’s crew. After you establish yourself in the race, Billy can invite you on a different job, Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab. Both titles appear in the Chapter 2 Story Recap. {src:1,2,3}
