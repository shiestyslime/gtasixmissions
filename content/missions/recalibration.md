## Get from Cal’s house to the fishing spot {#fishing-spot}

Pick up a six-pack at the liquor store on the way to Cal’s. At his house, take the boat to the marked fishing spot. The fishing controls become relevant when the catch objective appears. {src:1,2}

## Casting works, but the fish gets away {#hook-and-reel}

On PlayStation, hold **L2** on the rod and cast with **R2**. Rotate the right stick while the lure is in the water. When you feel the bite, press **R2** again to hook the fish. Push the left stick against the fish’s movement and rotate the right stick to bring it in. {src:1,2}

## When does the grill objective finish? {#grill-the-fish}

Take the catch back to Cal’s dock. At the grill, turn the pieces so both sides cook. Once the cooking segment ends and Lucia walks away, follow her and return home together. {src:1,2}
