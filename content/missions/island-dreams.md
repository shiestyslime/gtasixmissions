## When can I put on the scuba gear? {#equip-scuba-gear}

Meet Kenton at his shop and take the boat to the marked dive site with the group. The scuba interaction appears on the boat when you reach the marker. Equip the gear there, then guide the swimmers through the underwater trench to the wreck. {src:1,2}

## What does a green detector signal mean? {#detector-signal}

Switch to the metal detector at the wreck. Move it across the search area and watch its light. A green flash brings you close enough to narrow the search. Swim toward the signal and use the pickup interaction when it appears on the seabed. {src:1,2}

## What do I do when the search objective changes? {#rescue-prompt}

When the game asks you to rescue Dobbs, turn back toward the wreck and find him caught there. Free him and head back toward Kenton’s boat with the group. {src:1,2}
