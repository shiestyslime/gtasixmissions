## Where is the plunger? {#find-the-plunger}

Meet Mandy at her house and follow her indoors. When Jason needs a tool, look in the bathroom for the plunger. Pick it up and take it back to the sink to start the repair. {src:1,2}

## Why did the sink prompt change? {#sink-controls}

The repair has two parts. For the first, click the right stick on the PlayStation controller as the **R3** prompt appears. When the view moves underneath the sink, follow the new pipe-repair prompt. Finish that interaction before heading outside. {src:1,2}
