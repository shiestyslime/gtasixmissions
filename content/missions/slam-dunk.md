## Who starts the Dr. Bill mission? {#meet-dunk}

Dunk is the contact Lucia met through Vicki in Girls’ Trip. When he gets in touch, head to his trailer in Port Gellhorn. Follow Dunk from there to meet Dr. Bill at the store. {src:1,2,3}

## Do I take Dunk straight to Jason? {#return-with-dunk}

The driving destination at the end is Dunk’s trailer. Drop him there. The game handles Lucia’s return to Jason in the following scene. {src:1,3}
