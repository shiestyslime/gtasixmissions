## Talk to your cellmates {#talk-to-your-cellmates}

If Lucia is still looking out the window, turn her toward someone inside the cell. On PS5, hold **L2** while facing them. Once the dialogue choices appear, use the button beside the reply you want. {src:1,2}

## Where does the guard take you? {#follow-the-guard}

Stay with the guard through the common area. Your destination is the office at the far end of the corridor. Entering it advances the mission. {src:1,2}

## At the counter {#counter-prompt}

When the scene gives you control at the counter, focus on the teller and use the **Square** prompt on PS5. Wait for the cash to appear, then use the pickup prompt. If another character interrupts, follow the button prompt on screen. {src:1,2}

## Return to the coffee shop {#coffee-shop-marker}

If this objective appears, follow the destination marker back toward the shop. A story scene can take over before you reach the door. {src:1,2}
