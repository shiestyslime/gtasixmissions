## Who starts the flight objective? {#start-the-flight}

Speak with Donnie, then find Brian at the nearby bar. Return to the plane and follow the objective to taxi into the water. Fly to the marked intercept point to meet Pilot Pete. {src:1,2}

## Which airstrip do I land at? {#two-airstrips}

Stay with Pete through the escort. When he reaches his strip, check the next marker. That is the landing spot for your plane. Once you’re down, continue with Donnie on foot. {src:1,2}

## How do I catch up at the end? {#catch-up-with-donnie}

When the objective changes to catching up with Donnie, get onto a motorcycle at the airstrip. Follow the new destination to the water to rejoin him. {src:1,2}
