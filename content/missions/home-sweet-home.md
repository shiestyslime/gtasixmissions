## Where is the mission marker? {#automatic-start}

This mission starts automatically after [Tourist Trap](/missions/tourist-trap/). You’re already playing the next part when control returns to Lucia at the house. Enter the building to start the first objective. {src:1,2}

## Do I keep looking inside the house? {#leave-the-house}

Watch the current objective during the indoor sequence. When the escape instruction replaces the original task, leave through the front of the house. Staying inside to keep searching delays the escape. {src:1,2}

## What starts the next mission? {#leave-the-area}

Continue until you are outside the search area. A scene takes the story straight into [Discharge Time](/missions/discharge-time/), the last mission of Chapter 1. {src:1,2}
