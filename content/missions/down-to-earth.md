## How clean does the boat have to be? {#wash-the-boat}

Find Brian at the marina and follow him to the boat. Use the pressure washer and work over the remaining patches of grime until the objective changes. Once Lucia joins you, follow the flight objective to the seaplane. {src:1,2}

## What should I do at the markers over water? {#seaplane-markers}

At Bobcat Bay, land beside the boat to start the first pickup. Later, follow the map markers and read each new objective. The final boat segment changes the instruction to flying alongside the moving boat. After the drop, return to the marked dock. {src:1,2}
