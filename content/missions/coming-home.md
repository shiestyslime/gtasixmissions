## Which errands are optional? {#optional-errands}

Before the pickup, you can buy groceries, flowers or a necklace, get a haircut and collect Lucia’s clothes. The errands affect the relationship and can be skipped. If you pick up her clothes, bring Jason’s car so the outfit can travel in the trunk. {src:1,2}

## Who do I speak to at the prison and the bar? {#pickup-and-drinks}

At the prison, speak to the guard to start the pickup. Drive Lucia to the Rusty Anchor. After ordering drinks at the bar, head outside to speak to Lucia. The deck is the next stop in that interaction. {src:1,2}

## How do I get past Hang Out With Your Friends? {#bar-interactions}

Talk to the people around the bar and follow the dance prompt when it appears. Later, find Mattia and speak to him to start pool. You only need to play until the scene changes. Winning a full game is unnecessary. {src:1,2}
