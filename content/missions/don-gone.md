## Find the bag before you pack {#bag-on-the-bed}

Enter Donnie’s trailer and go to the bed. Pick up the bag there, then work through the belongings inside the trailer. {src:1,2}

## Which items am I missing? {#belongings-checklist}

- **Flight schedule** — look beside the bed near the Patriot Beer neon sign. {src:1,2}
- **Photo** — look up at the wall beside the TV. {src:1,2}
- **Map** — check the fridge. {src:1,2}
- **Clothes** — search the clothing storage across from the bed. {src:1,2}
- **Donnie’s ID** — check the shelving in the trailer. {src:1,2}

After packing the belongings, go outside. The next driving objective uses Donnie’s car and leads out of Key Lento. {src:1,2}
