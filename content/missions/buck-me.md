## Where do I go for Buck Me? {#bank-visit}

The banking visit takes place inside Fleeca. Approach the teller behind the counter first. She directs you to the manager. {src:1,2}

## Speak to the manager {#speak-to-manager}

After the teller's instructions, follow the manager into his office. The conversation there leads into the next part of the setup. {src:1,2}

## Set up banking on your phone {#open-buckme}

When control returns at Jason's home, open BuckMe on the phone and follow the savings tutorial. The setup continues at home after the bank visit. {src:3,4}

## FAQ

### Why do I not have a bank account yet?

Banking unlocks through Buck Me. Before this mission, you do not yet have an account. {src:3,5}

### Does the banking setup finish inside Fleeca?

No. The bank conversation comes first; the phone tutorial follows at Jason's home. {src:3,4}
