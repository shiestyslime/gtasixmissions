## Where does Breakfast Run start? {#check-on-jason}

After the medicine errand with Dunk, Lucia returns to Jason. When the call comes and the motel is marked, head to the room. The breakfast outing begins after the scene there. {src:1,3}

## What follows the diner? {#after-the-diner}

Follow the joint story objectives after arriving at Hank’s Waffles. Breakfast Run continues across several locations. The next named mission is Coffee Break, back at the motel with Jason and Lucia. {src:1,2,3}
