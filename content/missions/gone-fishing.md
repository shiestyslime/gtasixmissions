## Cal will not collect a package {#package-pickup}

Ease alongside the package instead of passing at speed. Keep the boat close enough for Cal's hook to reach it. {src:1,2}

## Getting through the camp {#camp-entry}

In the camp, go to the target building’s front door. It is unlocked. Use the entry prompt there. {src:3,4}

## Choosing the escape route {#optional-cash}

The trailer cash is optional. If you skip the trailer, you can keep going to the plane. {src:5,6}

## FAQ

### Must I clear every enemy before leaving?

No. You can run to the plane without defeating everyone. {src:5,6}

### Do I have to collect the trailer money?

No. Skipping that detour still lets you continue the mission. {src:5,6}
