## Open the shop menu {#andrews-counter}

After Gone Fishing, drive to Chuck’s Tackle & Shot as Jason. Go inside and face Andrew behind the counter. Hold **L2 on PlayStation** or **LT on Xbox** to bring up the interaction, then use the shop prompt. {src:1,3}

## Which item advances the objective? {#choose-a-longarm}

Look in the longarms selection. The mission accepts a shotgun or a tactical rifle, so you can choose whichever fits Jason’s current balance. Leave the shop when you finish buying. The mission continues outside. {src:1,2,3}

## Short on in-game cash? {#mailbox}

Check the mailbox outside Jason’s house for an uncollected payout before returning to the shop. The amount available depends on what you’ve already collected. {src:2,4}
