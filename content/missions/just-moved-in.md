## Which gas station counts for Just Moved In? {#find-alpha}

Use the gas station facing the motel. Enter its convenience store as Lucia. The shopping objective takes place inside among the shelves and refrigerators. {src:1,2,3}

## Where do I take the shopping? {#return-to-room}

Once you’ve collected the supplies, cross back to Starlet Motel and return to the room where Jason is staying. That handoff finishes the errand. {src:1,3}
