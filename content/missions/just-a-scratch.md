## Which vehicle do I bring to Joey? {#bring-the-ganado}

Drive Jason’s own pickup, the **’70 Ganado**, to the **Fix Your Car** marker. It leads to Coastal Auto Care. Stop outside the garage in the pickup and speak to Joey before driving through the open door. {src:1,2,3}

## How do I get past Look at Joey? {#look-at-joey}

Hold **Up on the D-pad** to focus on Joey. While the greeting is available, press **Cross on PS5** or **A on Xbox**. After he speaks, drive the Ganado into the workshop to bring up the repair menu. {src:1,2}

## How do I restore the engine and respray the Ganado? {#restore-and-respray}

1. Open **Full Engine** and apply the stock engine restoration. This mission covers the repair. {src:1,3}
2. Switch to **Cosmetics**, open **Respray** and confirm a color marked **Free**. The engine repair and the paint job are separate menu choices. {src:1,2,3}
3. Once you have applied the paint, exit the shop to finish. You can leave the optional upgrades for a later visit. {src:2,3,4}

## FAQ

### Is one paint color required?

You can choose the color. Pick a free option from the respray menu to finish without spending Jason’s cash. {src:1,2,3}

### What does the repair mission unlock?

After this visit, you can use Jason’s phone to call for help with the Ganado when it needs repairs. You can also return to Coastal Auto Care. {src:3,4}
