## Why can’t I carry the first package? {#riverbank-package}

The first package is a clue. Walk to the water’s edge and examine it. Leave the bank once Jason has inspected it, then use the boat to reach the marked shack farther downriver. {src:1,2}

## Where does the second package go? {#shack-and-dropoff}

Enter the shack and collect the package inside. The delivery takes you to Donnie’s trailer. Go to the marked container there and deposit the bag to complete the drop. {src:1,2}
