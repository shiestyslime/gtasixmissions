## Which motel room is Vicki in? {#room-ten}

Start by asking around at Starlet Motel as Lucia. Zach is outside between your room and reception. After talking to him, go to Room 10 and knock on Vicki’s door. {src:1,3}

## Where does Vicki take me next? {#follow-vicki}

When Vicki agrees to help, continue with her into Port Gellhorn. Her workplace, Delights Cabaret, is a stop along the way. Dunk is the contact she introduces later in the same mission. {src:1,2,3}
