## Which vehicle do I need at the camp? {#find-the-helicopter}

Ride to the camp in Brian’s truck and follow him on foot after you arrive. When the escape sequence begins, stay with him through the camp. Once you reach the helicopter, board it to continue. {src:1,2}

## Why doesn’t the boat jump appear? {#boat-jump}

If the mission reaches the boat chase, bring your boat directly alongside the one you are following. The transfer happens at short range. Keep pace beside it until the game offers the jump interaction, then follow the on-screen prompt. {src:1,2}
