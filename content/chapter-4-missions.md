## Ocean Beach {#ocean-beach}

At the end of The Cartel Heist, choosing Ocean Beach from the prompt moves you into Chapter 4. Pick the hangout option if you want to spend more time there first. Your new base is the Y Hotel. {src:2,3}

## Another Life {#another-life}

This is Lucia’s flashback after Ocean Beach. If the setting changes abruptly, you are playing the second Chapter 4 story entry. Company Cars is the next vehicle pickup in the Story Recap. {src:4,5}

## Pool Day {#pool-day}

In the Y Hotel suite, find Lucia outside on the balcony. Agree to go down to the pool together. Later, meeting Eddie at the bar leads to a separate party invitation. [See when to follow Eddie’s text](/side-missions/#pool-day). {src:3}

## Lawyer Up {#lawyer-up}

Look for **Meet Abe Gonzalez**. Lawyer Up is the first meeting with the lawyer. There is also a later **Pay Abe’s Fee** task, and the Chapter 5 meeting with Abe has its own title. {src:1,4}

## Party Hard {#party-hard}

Look for **Work Personal Security**. This is the celebrity-security job for both leads. A later marker mentioning security for Andrés specifically starts Adventures in Babysitting as Jason. {src:1,4}

## Photo/Shoot {#photo-shoot}

Choose **Shoot a Promo** for Dre’Quan’s photo session with Jason and Lucia. If the objective says to help with the launch party instead, that is Headliners, a different Dimez job. {src:1,4}

## Blood Sweat and Gear {#blood-sweat-and-gear}

For the **Work Out** marker, switch to Lucia. PowerPyx groups this training task with the airport preparations. The later **Workout** entry in the same chain is Amateur Hour. {src:1}

## Amateur Hour {#amateur-hour}

Use Lucia for this gym marker. The next contact in this job is Cisco. It shares similar workout wording with Blood Sweat and Gear, so compare the contact or active mission title before following a guide. {src:1,4}

## Cash Injections {#cash-injections}

Use Lucia for Nate’s **Hang Out With Nate** marker. If the current objective is simply to call him, the call is a separate preparation earlier in the list. {src:1}

## Dirty Work {#dirty-work}

Look for Raina’s objective in the airport preparations. The map can say **Deal with Raina**. The story recap uses **Dirty Work** for this part of Jason and Lucia’s work with Kordell’s crew. {src:1,4}

## Child’s Play {#childs-play}

Look for Rafa’s rescue objective after the security work for Andrés. The lead-up **Rafa’s Kidnapping** is listed separately as a Jason-only stage. {src:1,5}

## This Is a Free Call From… {#this-is-a-free-call-from}

When Lucia gets the optional pickup for **Shayna**, take it while the marker is active. PowerPyx identifies this as a time-limited job that can disappear. {src:1}

## Power Play {#power-play}

The marker reads **Help Kordell’s Crew**. PowerPyx lists Lucia as the character who can start it. PC Gamer describes Jason doing the information-gathering in the mission. If Jason has no start marker, check Lucia’s map. {src:1,4}

## Team Building {#team-building}

Use Lucia for **Meet Kordell**. On the same preparation chain, **Help Andrea** belongs to Jason and starts Delivery Method. Switch according to the name on your phone. {src:1}

## VCIA Takedown {#vcia-takedown}

Look for the airport heist start after the **Heist Plans in Place** entry. If your current marker still mentions a catering truck, you are on Fully Catered, one of the preparations. Make a separate [manual save](/save-game/) during free roam if you want to revisit this point. {src:1,6}

## Call Nate {#call-nate}

Switch to Lucia if this preparation is waiting. PowerPyx lists the phone call and the later meetup with Nate as separate entries. Once the map says to hang out with him, use the [Cash Injections entry](#cash-injections). {src:1}

## Rafa’s Kidnapping {#rafas-kidnapping}

Check Jason’s map after Adventures in Babysitting. PowerPyx lists the follow-up with Rafa as a Jason-only stage, before the later rescue in Child’s Play. {src:1}

## It’s A Date! {#its-a-date}

PowerPyx places this date-night interlude between Lawyer Up and Party Hard. If your map is already asking you to work personal security, jump to [Party Hard](#party-hard) for the next assignment. {src:1,7}

## Pay Abe’s Fee {#chapter-4-pay-abes-fee}

This is Abe’s first payment task. GTA Base also lists a separate payment in Chapter 5, so save the later checkmark even after you pay him in this chapter. {src:1,8}

## Bad Driver! {#bad-driver}

The objective here is to find **Felipe**. PowerPyx lists Bad Driver as an optional job. If you are following only airport preparations, look for the Kordell crew markers elsewhere in this list. {src:1,9}

## Nate Will Be in Touch {#nate-will-be-in-touch}

Continue the available jobs for Kordell’s crew while waiting for Nate to get back to you. The separate **Call Nate** task asks Lucia to place the call herself. {src:1,10}

## Heist Plans in Place {#heist-plans-in-place}

This marks the point where the airport preparations are ready. To begin the operation itself, look for **Start the Airport Heist** and use the [VCIA Takedown entry](#vcia-takedown). {src:1,11}

## Tie Up Loose Ends {#tie-up-loose-ends}

This comes after the airport heist in PowerPyx’s chapter list. The café job **That’s Where the Money Is…** is a separate optional task. If your phone asks you to return Valentina’s call, follow that prompt instead. {src:1,12}

## Call Valentina Back {#call-valentina-back}

This is an outgoing call. Contact Valentina when the objective appears. The next front-desk visit is a different entry named **Sunset**. {src:1,13}
