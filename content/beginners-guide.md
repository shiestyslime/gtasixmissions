## What is new in GTA 6 compared with GTA 5?

| Topic | GTA 5 | GTA 6 |
|---|---|---|
| Setting | Los Santos, San Andreas | Vice City, Leonida {confirmed} |
| Protagonists | Three | Two, Jason and Lucia {confirmed} |
| Wanted stars | Five | Six {reported} |
| Money | One balance | Bank, carried cash and stolen goods {reported} |
| Story gates | Missions only | Missions plus cash totals {reported} |
| Combat | Cover shooting | Heavier, closer to Red Dead Redemption 2 {reported} |
| Frame rate on console | 30 fps at launch | 30 fps target on every console {confirmed} |
| Length | About 30 hours for the story | About 80 hours in one Rockstar developer's playthrough {reported} |
| Boxed copy | Disc | Download code, no disc {reported} |

## Before you play

1. Check the free space on your console before downloading. Rockstar lists **up to 212 GB** of available space, depending on your device. See [release date and file size](/release-date/).
2. Update your console.
3. Choose your edition. The Standard Edition is the full game. See [price and editions](/editions-price/).

Before you start a new mission, [make a separate save](/save-game/). This gives you a place to return to if you want to try the mission again.

## Your first hours

At the **Navigation Experience** screen, pick **Always On** if you want the map visible throughout your first drive. If you want it to fade when you’re exploring, choose a dynamic preset. See [all three minimap settings and how to change them](/navigation-settings/).

1. **Start with the Prologue.** If the first conversation does not start, open the [mission guides](/missions/) and search for “talk to your cellmates.”
2. **Stash your cash.** Banking opens later. Until then, use the [floor stash in Jason’s bedroom](/money-making/#early-cash-stash).
3. **Sell loot soon.** Stolen goods are worth nothing until you reach a fence.
4. **Save some money.** Later chapters may need it.
5. **Explore between missions.** The open world is where extra income comes from.
6. **Start small.** {reported} Jason and Lucia begin with small jobs, such as hotwiring cheap cars and picking simple locks. Some parked cars need tools that you get later.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Carrying a lot of cash into a hard mission
- Holding loot for a long time before selling it
- Spending everything on cars and clothes in the first hours
- Trusting "full walkthrough" or "money glitch" pages published before launch

## FAQ

### Do I need to play GTA 5 before GTA 6?

No. GTA 6 has a new story, new characters and a new setting.

### Is GTA 6 single-player?

Yes. GTA 6 has a single-player story. Rockstar has not yet detailed an online mode.

### Can I listen to the GTA 6 radio on foot?

{confirmed} Yes. Jason and Lucia can listen to radio stations or on-demand podcasts through headphones or earbuds connected to their in-game phones. Both are also available in vehicles. {src:8}

### Does GTA 6 run at 60 fps?

No. Rockstar says GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. Reports say there is no 60 fps mode at launch. The PS5 Pro adds a sharper image and ray-traced shadows, not a higher frame rate.

### How long is GTA 6?

Rockstar's Rob Nelson said his own playthrough took about 80 hours. That included the main story and some optional goals. Rockstar has not given an official length.

### What age rating is GTA 6?

GTA 6 is rated for adults, like earlier games in the series. Check the rating on your store page for your country.
