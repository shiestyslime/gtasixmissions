## Transcript and visual description {#transcript}

A yellow cab moves along a green diagram of a city route. Each receipt shows how the fare changes. The clip uses on-screen text and brief electronic chimes.

- **0:00–0:04** “Skip the ride? That’s $100.” The receipt shows the regular fare plus a $100 skip.
- **0:04–0:08.5** “Keep the cash. Ride it out.” The standard trip receipt shows the regular fare with no skip fee.
- **0:08.5–0:13** “In a hurry? Pay to skip.” Talk to the driver to skip ahead. The receipt adds $100.
- **0:13–0:18** “Same trip. Your call.” Riding it out saves $100. The end card points to gtasixmissions.com.

## Catch your first ride

After [Gone Fishing](/missions/gone-fishing/), look for Paradise Taxi in the phone’s full contacts list. Set a destination before you call. If you have the RydeMe app, you can also request a car there. [Open the taxi and transit guide](/map-locations/#fast-travel). {src:1,2}
