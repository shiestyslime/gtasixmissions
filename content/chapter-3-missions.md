## How do I use this guide?

Match your current mission to a heading. Each entry gives you a specific place, menu or story trigger and links to the walkthrough behind it. If more than one contact is available, your route through the chapter can differ. To track your own order, [mark completed missions on the checklist](/missions/#catalogue-chapter-3).

## Welcome to Vice City {#welcome-to-vice-city}

Keep driving after Checking Out. At the Jack of Hearts, speak to the front-door bouncer first. Dre’Quan meets you at the entrance on the other side. {src:2}

## Dealer’s Choice {#dealers-choice}

Go upstairs inside the Jack of Hearts. The door at the top leads to the studio. Wait by the recording booth until Dre’Quan asks which hook you like. Either answer moves the conversation along. {src:3}

## Dubious Provenance {#dubious-provenance}

Start at home. Interact with the bag inside the Bayside apartment closet to reveal the antique store destination in Capri. {src:4}

## Mami Knows Best {#mami-knows-best}

Switch to Lucia for Mia’s marker in Peacock Bay. Once Mia finishes looking around the house in Hamlet, go back to the entrance to help her. {src:5}

## Tyrone {#tyrone}

This is Jason’s introduction to further work with Boobie’s contact, Tyrone. If your current objective already sends you to find Charles, look under Here, Kitty Kitty instead. {src:1}

## Here, Kitty Kitty {#here-kitty-kitty}

Start as Jason at Tyrone’s apartment complex in La Perle. In Charles’s Rockridge bedroom, inspect the receipt from Aline’s Thrift Store to get a lead without paying the man at the door. {src:6}

## Kitty Litter Labs {#kitty-litter-labs}

Look inside the purple search areas on the game map. The building you want has pink gas rising from it. The motel belongs to a later job called Spin On This. {src:1,7}

## Spin On This {#spin-on-this}

Go to the diner outside Grayson to meet Tyrone first. The next marker is the motel to the east. At the motel, the story sends you downstairs to the green door. {src:1,8}

## Boobie Time {#boobie-time}

Bring Jason alone to the Jack of Hearts. When Boobie exits, take the driver’s seat in his car. Wait at each pickup until his conversation finishes and you get another marker. {src:9}

## Meet the Heders {#meet-the-heders}

After Dealer’s Choice, check the Bayside apartment for Cal’s visit about the hard drive. Once Brian gets in touch, the outfit bag in the bedroom starts your next outing. {src:10}

## Car Service {#car-service}

Look for the text from a blocked number after Meet the Heders. The marker you need is in Salton, in southern Vice City. {src:11}

## Top Billing {#top-billing}

At the Jack of Hearts, take the hallway to the right of the stairs for backstage. Later, if you lose track of the Dimez, ask the host near the front door and check the parking lot. {src:12}

## First Impressions {#first-impressions}

Bring both characters to Waxman Island. Use **2404** on the garage keypad, then enter the car as Lucia to get the next destination. {src:13}

## Crosstown Traffic {#crosstown-traffic}

There are two starting points. Lucia can meet Raul at the Courtyard Bar in Stockyard. Jason can meet Karim at the La Perle boatyard. Either one starts the mission. {src:14}

## Credit Check {#credit-check}

Take both characters to the marker near Vice Vinyl. Use the wall intercom at the entrance to meet the crew. {src:15}

## Clonin’ Keys, Makin’ GS {#clonin-keys-makin-gs}

At Hathaway Apartments in Ocean Beach, check the ground level on the east side of the in-game garage for Randy’s Zorrusso. The nearby story prompt adds the app you need to the in-game phone. {src:16}

## Ride Or Die {#ride-or-die}

After Dre’Quan texts, bring Jason alone to the Jack of Hearts. Use the ledge outside to wait. At the Eris store, go upstairs and find the shopper with a bag near the DJ. {src:17}

## Sinfrontera National Bank {#sinfrontera-national-bank}

The bank is in Tequesta. Once inside as Lucia, follow Jason to the table. Use the interaction to lean there and wait for the next objective. {src:18}

## Security Expert {#security-expert}

Keep playing after Sinfrontera National Bank. The transition takes you to Waning Sands automatically. For the first objective, follow Jason’s directions past the marked house and wait for the parking prompt. {src:19}

## Caught Slipping {#caught-slipping}

Try entering the Bayside apartment after Boobie Time and First Impressions. The event starts at the apartment entrance itself. {src:20}

## Company Property {#company-property}

After Security Expert, go to Skeeter’s in Hamlet. Speak to Brian, then follow him outside to his truck to get the airfield destination. {src:21}

## Safe Word {#safe-word}

Go back to the compound in Stockyard after Company Property. Speak to Nicole inside. The truck waiting outside is your mission vehicle. {src:22}

## The Missing Link {#the-missing-link}

Bring Lucia on her own to Sloppy Molly’s in Watson Bay. Inside the next building, wait for Andrea’s text before going to the north door, near the bouncer in white. {src:23}

## Cooties {#cooties}

Wait for Dre’Quan’s invitation after Ride or Die. His music-video shoot starts in Crosstown. You control Lucia during filming. {src:24}

## Hidden Charges {#hidden-charges}

The planner can show **Prepping the Explosives** before the job is ready. Finish the other available story objectives, including Cooties, and watch for Karim’s invitation to the planning room. {src:25}

## Bricked Up {#bricked-up}

When Boobie contacts you, return to Bayside. If the parking objective at Venture Apartments stays on screen, move the car outside the red area before getting out. {src:26}

## South Leonida Real Estate {#south-leonida-real-estate}

After Mia follows up with Lucia, go to her office in Hamlet. Read the note on the door. That interaction sets your destination to the house. {src:27}

## The Cartel Heist {#the-cartel-heist}

When Raul calls after the preparations, meet at the planning room. At the end of the mission, you can hang out for a while or choose Ocean Beach to enter Chapter 4. {src:28}

## Join a Street Race {#join-a-street-race}

After Dealer’s Choice, check Jason’s phone for a group message from Manuel. It adds a race meetup to the map. {src:29}

## Buddy? {#buddy}

Complete both McKittrick Farm and Kyle’s chemical errand first. His next marker comes in a text. PowerPyx still had time to do other Chapter 3 jobs before the message arrived. {src:30}

## Yasmani’s Jewelry Jobs {#yasmanis-jewelry-jobs}

After Boobie Time, look for a message from Yasmani. His three shop objectives can be tackled in any order. Return to him after the first pickup for the in-game reward. {src:31}

## Bumper to Bumper {#bumper-to-bumper}

Finish Join a Street Race first. If Yago’s marker has a lock icon, return in the afternoon or evening. Sleeping at the apartment moves the game clock forward. {src:32}

## Race Against Yago {#race-against-yago}

Talk to Santiago and pick Freeway Run. The hourglass on Yago’s invitation means it can expire. [See the missable missions guide](/side-missions/#yago). {src:33}

## Mall Rats {#mall-rats}

After Billy’s first two jobs, watch for his Ocean Beach marker. Bring one character alone. Arriving together prevents this mission from starting. {src:34}

## Brother To Brother {#brother-to-brother}

After Yago texts, take Jason to El Rincon Notorio. The interaction to start the job is at the restaurant’s back door. {src:35}

## Encrypted Hard Drive {#encrypted-hard-drive}

Look for Cal at the Bayside apartment after Dealer’s Choice. He comes to examine the hard drive there. If you have just finished the previous job, let some in-game time pass before checking back. {src:10}

## Heist Planning {#heist-planning}

If preparations are still showing, finish the other available Chapter 3 story work, including Hidden Charges. Wait for Raul to call, then go to the planning room. His call is the trigger described in the Cartel Heist walkthrough. {src:28}

## Get Your Wheels {#get-your-wheels}

Looking for the first vehicle task after the move? This marker is for getting a ride in Vice City. PowerPyx lists it as optional, so you can leave it on the map while following your story contacts. {src:36,37}

## Send Money to Lorena {#send-money-to-lorena}

This is the first of two separate Chapter 3 requests from Lorena. When you have sent the first payment in-game, check off this entry. Keep the later **Send More Money to Lorena** entry for her follow-up. {src:38}

## Send More Money to Lorena {#send-more-money-to-lorena}

Lorena asks for a second transfer in Chapter 3. Seeing her name again after making the first payment is part of the chapter. Track this follow-up separately from the earlier request. {src:39}

## Raul’s Next Job {#rauls-next-job}

Wait for Raul’s incoming call about new work. For the later appointment that asks both leads to pick up a car on Waxman Island, use [First Impressions](#first-impressions). The **2404** keypad belongs to that later job. {src:13,40}
