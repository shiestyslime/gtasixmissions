## Doggie Bag or VC Baby? {#vc-baby}

Check the contact. **Talk to Mattia** starts VC Baby. **Meet Vinny** is the marker for Doggie Bag. Mission lists place the two in different orders, so use the contact shown in your game to choose the guide. {src:4,5}

## Operation Savage Glory {#operation-savage-glory}

This is the shipment mission in Giorgio’s part of Chapter 5. After it, look for the objective to see Abe. If your phone still says to meet Vinny, that marker belongs to Doggie Bag. {src:4,5,6}

## Honest Abe {#honest-abe}

Look for **Negotiate a Deal with Abe**. There is another meeting with the same contact later. The **Meet with Abe** marker belongs to Protective Custody. {src:4}

## When should I keep a finale save? {#the-end-of-the-road}

During free roam, pause the game and choose **Game → Save Game**. Use an empty manual slot. Kotaku’s reviewer encountered a prompt to finish the story, and found that play returned to that point after the credits. An earlier manual save gives you another way back if you want to replay part of the build-up. {src:1,2}

## What can I do after the last mission? {#postgame}

Open **Progress → Stats → 100% Completion** to pick an unfinished activity. Collectibles, optional tasks and free roam are available after the story. For an earlier finished story mission, use a save from before it. [Check how loading a slot affects the autosave](/save-game/#load-an-older-save). {src:1,2,3}

## Unfinished Business {#unfinished-business}

PowerPyx places this short entry between Honest Abe and the second fee payment. If the instruction on screen already mentions paying Abe, use the next entry on your checklist. {src:4,7}

## Pay Abe’s Fee {#chapter-5-pay-abes-fee}

This is a second payment in the final chapter. A completed Chapter 4 payment can stay checked. For the subsequent meeting, the map label **Meet with Abe** refers to Protective Custody. {src:4,8}
