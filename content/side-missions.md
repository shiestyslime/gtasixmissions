## What can I miss in Chapter 1? {#shirley}

Return to the houses in **Key Lento** after Pipework becomes available. Listen for Shirley calling to Jason and speak to her for the rum errand. Once you deliver it, let roughly a day pass in-game and come back for her second favor involving Peggy’s scooter. Finish both before accepting Mattia’s offer in **Tourist Trap**. PowerPyx says neither is needed for the overall completion checklist. {src:1,2}

## Yago invited me to race. Which event do I pick? {#yago}

After Bumper to Bumper, look for a message to Jason and an hourglass on the Vice City race marker. Go to the meetup while the invitation is active, talk to Santiago and choose **Freeway Run**, the second race. Other events at the meetup are easy to mistake for Yago’s. {src:3}

## Why can’t I start Kirk’s next mission? {#kirk}

Finish the Skunk Ape search and the tree effigies first. The later Mr. Mangrove job needs **Lucia at night**. If you met Kirk as Jason in Chapter 1, progress into Chapter 2 and watch for Kirk’s text when Lucia becomes available. {src:4,5}

## Pool Day and Eddie’s party (Chapter 4 spoilers) {#pool-day}

At the **Y Hotel**, talk to Lucia on the balcony and accept the trip to the pool. Picking up a drink at the bar introduces Eddie. His party invitation arrives by text later. For the party marker, arrive at the **Heathcliff Heights** lobby with one character. Either lead can go alone. {src:6}

## Pilot Pete’s hangar in Chapter 4 {#pilot-pete}

After Pool Day, look for a phone call to Jason. Go to the airfield marker in **Leonard County**, then find the red door inside the hangar. The in-game safe in Pete’s back room uses **3718**. This is a different keypad from the [two Lab Fees doors](/missions/lab-fees/). {src:7}

## Where are the other side missions?

The [chapter checklist](/missions/) includes optional campaign objectives alongside the story. For a particular side character, the [Strangers & Freaks walkthrough index](https://www.powerpyx.com/gta-6-all-strangers-freaks-side-missions/) groups the individual jobs under their starting chapter. Some contacts call back several game days later. {src:5}
