## Who do you play as in GTA 6?

{confirmed} You play as Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Lucia is the first female lead in a main Grand Theft Auto game since the series went 3D.

{reported} Previews describe it as one story told from two sides, not separate stories.

## The two protagonists

### Jason Duval

{confirmed} Jason grew up around crime and spent time in the Army. He now works for local drug runners in the Leonida Keys.

### Lucia Caminos

{confirmed} Lucia has just left Leonida Penitentiary. She wants a better life for herself and her family, and she teams up with Jason.

## Confirmed supporting characters

| Character | Who they are |
|---|---|
| Cal Hampton | Jason's friend and another of Brian's associates. He likes conspiracy theories and listens in on Coast Guard radio from home. |
| Boobie Ike | A Vice City businessman with property, a strip club and a recording studio. |
| Dre'Quan Priest | A former dealer who now runs a music label with Boobie. |
| Real Dimez | A rap duo, Bae-Luxe and Roxy, signed to that label. |
| Raul Bautista | A seasoned bank robber who is always looking for new talent to take on risky jobs. |
| Brian Heder | A veteran drug runner who still moves product through his boat yard in the Keys. |

{confirmed} All six appear on Rockstar's official site.

## Official story setup (light spoilers) {#story-setup}

**Spoiler note:** this section describes the opening premise from Rockstar's official site. It does not cover missions, choices or how the story ends.

- {confirmed} In Rockstar's published premise, a job that should have been easy goes wrong. Jason and Lucia end up caught in a criminal conspiracy that reaches across the state of Leonida, and they have to depend on each other.
- {confirmed} Lucia's father trained her to fight from a young age. Fighting for her family put her in Leonida Penitentiary, and she got out by luck. She wants the good life her mother has hoped for since the family lived in Liberty City.
- {confirmed} Jason grew up around crooks and served in the Army. He now works for drug runners in the Keys and stays rent-free at a property owned by Brian Heder, in return for helping with local shakedowns.
- {confirmed} Raul Bautista is a veteran bank robber who keeps looking for people willing to take big risks for big rewards.
- For help with the first playable scenes, start with the [opening mission guides](/missions/).

## Can you switch between Jason and Lucia?

{reported} Yes. Previews show both characters in missions together, and some missions let you choose a role, such as driving or shooting, while your partner does the other job.

{reported} Rockstar told The Guardian that you can switch between Jason and Lucia most of the time. Some story moments lock you to one of them.

## Jason and Lucia's relationship

- {reported} When the two are apart, they can call and text each other.
- {reported} They can go on dates. A date can even be a crime, such as robbing a liquor store together.
- {reported} Rockstar's Rob Nelson says you can look after the relationship or let it get worse.
- {unknown} What the relationship changes in the story or in gameplay.

## FAQ

### Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. They are a couple and both are playable.

### Is there a female protagonist in GTA 6?

Yes. Lucia Caminos is a playable lead character.

### Can you switch characters in GTA 6?

Yes, according to Rockstar's interview with The Guardian. You can switch between Jason and Lucia most of the time, but some story moments lock one of them.

### How many protagonists does GTA 6 have?

Two: Jason and Lucia. GTA 5 had three.

### Who are the voice actors for Jason and Lucia?

The Game Awards nominated **Manni L. Perez** for her GTA VI performance, and GamesRadar identifies her role as Lucia Caminos. We have not verified Jason’s actor from the credits. Stephen Root separately confirmed to the Associated Press that he appears in the game. {src:7,8,9}
