## Not enough space

**Check the drive GTA 6 is installing to.** Free space on two drives cannot be pooled for one install. Use the required space shown by your console. PlayStation lists 212 GB of free space for the PS5 preload. Rockstar says the requirement is up to 212 GB, depending on the device. Transfer and installed sizes can differ.

1. **PS5** — Open **Settings → Storage**. If you have two playable drives, check **Installation Location** and the free space on that drive. Move other games to an existing drive or remove games and captures you no longer need. Work in **Games and Apps** or **Media Gallery** and leave the **Saved Data** category alone.
2. **Xbox Series X or S** — Open **My games & apps → Manage → Storage devices** and check your intended install drive. Open **Manage → Free up space** to find leftover add-ons and other removals. Move or uninstall old game installs and leave saved data alone.
3. **Check again before buying a drive.** If your console still asks for space, use the number on the current prompt. If there is already enough space on the selected drive, [check the download queue and account](/launch-help/#game-missing-or-locked).

### Compatible storage if you still need it

- **PS5** — A compatible PCIe Gen4 x4 M.2 NVMe drive with a heatsink can install and run PS5 games. The [WD_BLACK SN850P with heatsink](aff:storage-ps5) is one manufacturer-listed example. A USB drive can store PS5 games for later transfer back to playable storage.
- **Xbox Series X or S** — A licensed Xbox storage expansion card can run Series X and S games. The [WD_BLACK C50 1 TB expansion card](aff:storage-xbox) is one manufacturer-listed example. An external USB drive can archive the install for later transfer back to playable storage.
- **Xbox One cloud** — Skip the storage purchase for this game. [Cloud streaming does not install GTA 6 on your Xbox One](/launch-help/#xbox-one-cloud-gaming).

## Boxed code not working

{confirmed} Boxed Standard copies contain a one-time download code. Redeem it on the console account and in the region you intend to play on. Enter the full code only through the official console store or platform redemption page.

### Code missing or unreadable

**Keep the box and proof of purchase and contact the retailer.** Check its replacement policy. PlayStation directs GTA 6 buyers with a missing or damaged code to the seller.

### Wrong region

**Compare the package’s redemption region with your platform account’s region.** Rockstar recommends buying in the country of your account. Ask the retailer about an exchange if the regions differ.

### Code already redeemed

**Check your transaction history on the account that redeemed the code.** Use [PlayStation transaction history](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/store/check-ps-store-transaction-subscription-service/) or [Microsoft order history](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/accounts-billing/view-your-microsoft-store-order-history). If the purchase appears there, follow the [library steps](/launch-help/#missing-after-redemption). If you cannot find it on any account that could have redeemed it, use the retailer’s or platform’s official support and supply proof of purchase privately.

### Ready to redeem

Use [PlayStation’s voucher steps](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/store/redeem-ps-store-voucher-code/) or [Microsoft’s redemption page](https://redeem.microsoft.com/). Keep your code out of public posts, photos and comments.

## Game missing or locked

### Missing after redemption

**Start with the account that redeemed the code.** On PS5, open **Game Library → Your Collection** and check **Downloads/Uploads**. On Xbox, check **My games & apps → Full library** on the purchasing account, then check the download queue. If the account still lacks the game after confirmed redemption, contact platform support with proof of purchase.

### Installed but locked

**Check the console store countdown before troubleshooting a lock.** A preload stays locked until your storefront unlocks it. After [release on your platform](/release-date/#when-does-gta-6-come-out), confirm the full download and current console software. On PS5, try [Restore Licenses](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/restore-licences-playstation/) on the account that owns it. For other errors, use [PlayStation download and launch support](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/gta-vi-game/) or [Xbox support](https://support.xbox.com/).

### PS5 download stuck or failing

{confirmed} PlayStation lists these steps for a GTA 6 download that stops or fails.

1. Open **Downloads/Uploads**, select the download, choose **Pause**, then **Resume**.
2. If it still fails, choose **Cancel and Delete**. Find the game in **Game Library** and start the download again.
3. Check the PlayStation Network status page, your system software version and the free space in **Settings → Storage**.
4. As a last step, start the console in Safe Mode and choose **Clear Cache and Rebuild Database**.

If the game is installed but will not start on a second console or account, PlayStation says to check that the account owns the game and that **Console Sharing and Offline Play** is enabled.

## Xbox One cloud gaming

{confirmed} Xbox lists GTA 6 for cloud streaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X and S from **1 a.m. Eastern on November 19, 2026**. On Xbox One, look for the cloud play option after the announced start. Native installs are available on Xbox Series X and S.

1. Confirm Xbox Cloud Gaming is supported for your account, console and market before spending money.
2. Buy GTA 6 separately. Use eligible Game Pass cloud access or purchased cloud playtime in supported markets. Check your console store for local availability and price.
3. Use the Xbox Cloud Gaming option on your console. A native install compatibility message on Xbox One is expected. Streaming from your own console with Remote Play is a separate feature.

## Offline play and service status

### Can you play GTA 6 offline on PS5?

{confirmed} Yes, on your main PS5. The U.S. PlayStation Store listing marks GTA VI as **offline play enabled**. It says you can play on the main PS5 linked to your account through the **Console Sharing and Offline Play** setting.

1. PlayStation turns this setting on for the first PS5 you sign in on.
2. On that PS5, go to **Settings → Users and Accounts → Other → Console Sharing and Offline Play** and select **Enable** if it is off.
3. Only one PS5 can have it on for your account at a time. To move it to another PS5, disable it on the previous console first, then enable it on the new one. On any other PS5, sign in with the account that owns the game.

You still need an internet connection to redeem a boxed code, download or preload the game, and get updates. The listing says online features need a PlayStation account.

### Can you play offline on Xbox?

Xbox Cloud Gaming needs an internet connection throughout play. For an installed digital copy, check the [Xbox offline setup instructions](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/help/hardware-network/connect-network/using-xbox-one-offline) before disconnecting. These cover the console’s home Xbox and license settings.

### Is it down? Check the official status pages

Open the official page for the service you use. This site does not show a live status, because a copy can be out of date.

- [PlayStation Network service status](https://status.playstation.com/): account management, gaming and social features, and PlayStation Store.
- [Xbox status](https://support.xbox.com/en-US/xbox-live-status): account and profile, store and subscriptions, and cloud gaming.
- [Rockstar Games service status](https://support.rockstargames.com/servicestatus): Rockstar sign-in and online services. On November 17 it listed GTA Online and shared Rockstar services, with no separate GTA VI entry.

### Who to contact

Match your problem to the right team before you wait in a queue.

1. **The game will not buy, redeem, download, install or start, or your account is the problem.** On PS5, PlayStation Support handles your account, codes, downloads, installs, game launch issues, refunds and suspensions. On Xbox, use [Xbox support](https://support.xbox.com/). Check the status pages above first.
2. **The game runs but crashes, a patch fails, or something looks like a bug.** Rockstar Support handles gameplay issues, patches and bugs.
3. **You are stuck on a mission or a checkpoint will not advance.** [Search the mission guides](/missions/) for the objective on screen. Report a bug to Rockstar Support if the game itself is broken.
